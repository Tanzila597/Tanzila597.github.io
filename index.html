About

Hi, I'm Tanzila.

I'm a marketing and communications professional with 8+ years of experience across Canada, the Middle East, and India. I hold a Post-Graduate Diploma in Digital Marketing and Marketing Analytics from Confederation College. I care most about work that's honest and community-first — never corporate-sounding for the sake of it.
